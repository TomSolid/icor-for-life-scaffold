# Video on why I switched to Obsidian

An idea is a thing you might do. No finish line, so it is not a Project. Not one of your three quarterly explorations, so it is not a Topic. It sits here collecting thoughts until it becomes one of them or you let it go.

## The idea in one line

What it is, said plainly enough that you will recognise it in six months.

## Thoughts

Everything you collect for it, newest at the bottom or wherever you like. Angles, hooks, objections, links, half-sentences. This section has no shape on purpose.

## What would make this real

The smallest version of this that could exist. When you can answer this, the idea is usually ready to become a Project.

## Decision

Left empty while `idea_status: open`. Filled when it changes:

- **promoted**: name the Project or Topic it became, and link it above.
- **parked**: why now is not the time, and what would change that.
- **dropped**: why you decided against it. This is the most valuable thing on the page, because it stops you having the same idea again next year.
