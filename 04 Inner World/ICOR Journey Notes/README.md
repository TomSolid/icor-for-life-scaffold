# ICOR Journey Notes

Your growth assignment reflections from app.myicor.com, synced into the
vault by the myICOR Connect plugin (the myICOR button under the folder
tree, or the command "Sync myICOR reflections").

- One note per reflection: the question, your answer verbatim, your notes,
  and the quality score in frontmatter (see [[GL-1002-frontmatter-conventions]]).
- The sync only CREATES notes. Once a reflection lands here it is yours:
  edit it, extend it, link it into your Journal, Topics, and Goals. The
  sync will never overwrite a local note.
- New reflections you write in myICOR arrive on the next sync.
