# Why I keep a Daily Scratchpad

Example note. A note has a subject rather than a date, so it lives here
and not in the Journal, and it keeps changing: this one is an outline I
add to whenever the reason for the habit gets clearer. It is filed under
the [[Knowledge Management]] topic, which is what makes it findable from
the thing it is about.

Every note in this folder carries at least one link to a Project, Key
Element or Topic. A note that links to nothing failed the Capturing
Beast and should not exist, so the link is not decoration: it is the
note's reason to be here.
