# Contacts

People and Companies, one note each, in `People/` and `Companies/`. A
contact note holds properties, not stories: role, relation, companies,
aliases, email, birthday, last contact, next action
([[GL-1002-frontmatter-conventions|GL-1002]]). What you write about a
person lives in the Journal and links back through `linked_people`.
People and companies link to each other through `companies` and
`people`.

Created by the team from
[[SOP-1005-create-or-update-a-contact|SOP-1005]], or by you: search the
name first (Cmd+O), then right-click `People/` or `Companies/`, New
note, insert the `person` or `company` template, fill the properties
([[GL-1007-capture-and-where-things-go|GL-1007]] "Doing it by hand").
One person, one note.

`People.base` and `Companies.base` show both folders as live tables;
edit properties right in the table.
